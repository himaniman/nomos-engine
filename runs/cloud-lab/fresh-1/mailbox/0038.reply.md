Then it gets caught at the bottom-centre, where the grey-blue track turns into a dense scribble against one block of rock: it paces back and forth along that face for a long stretch and earns only a single meal there. It escapes north on a wide loop through the top rooms (three more meals). It spends its red old age wound into one small walled room right of centre, circling tightly around a spot where it has already eaten, with short excursions south-east that always come back. The mechanism I would fix first is a slow-fading "trodden" trace. Every cell the agent stands on would be marked, the mark would fade over a few hundred ticks, and it would divide both the injection and the frontier sources.
